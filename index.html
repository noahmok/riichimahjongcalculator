Closed One Han

Menzinchin tsumohou (tsumo): Any closed hand that wins by self draw.


In this example, you draw a 4 of characters on your turn with this hand. Since you have accomplished the minimum requirement of 4 valid pons and/or chis and an eyes, you have achieved Menzinchin Tsumo by drawing into your winning tile in a closed hand.
